### 🔒 Featured Project

<table>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <h3>CloakBid — Privacy-Preserving Sealed-Bid Auctions</h3>
      <p>
        <strong>CloakBid</strong> explores a privacy-preserving auction protocol built on <strong>Midnight</strong>, designed to keep bid information confidential while still enabling verifiable, trust-minimized auction outcomes.
      </p>
      <h4>⚠️ The Problem</h4>
      <p>
        Traditional public blockchain auctions expose transaction values and bid timing to the entire network. This transparency creates vulnerabilities such as front-running, predatory bid sniping, and premature leakage of sensitive pricing data.
      </p>
      <h4>💡 The Approach</h4>
      <p>
        CloakBid addresses these challenges by exploring confidential bidding with privacy-centric blockchain architecture. Bids remain strictly confidential throughout the bidding phase, while cryptographic verification ensures that the winning bid and auction resolution can be settled and audited without compromising bidder privacy.
      </p>
      <h4>🛠️ Tech & Concepts</h4>
